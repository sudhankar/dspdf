How to watermark a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Choose text or image watermark.
  3. Adjust font, size, color, opacity, rotation, and position (or tile).
  4. Click Apply watermark.

Technical limitations

  • Watermarks are baked into the PDF page content and cannot be removed afterwards. Keep a backup of the original.
  • Rotation is applied to text via pdf-lib's degrees rotation, which rotates around the anchor point.
  • Image watermarks support PNG and JPG.

Frequently asked

Can I remove a watermark later?

Not with this tool. Watermarks become part of the page content. Always keep a backup of your original PDF.

What's the best rotation?

45° for diagonal, 0° for horizontal center, 90° for vertical side text.

Does "Tile" fill the whole page?

Yes — it repeats the watermark in a grid covering the page.

Privacy

Watermark PDF processes the selected PDF in your browser rather than sending it to a DSPDF PDF-processing server. Your browser may load public libraries needed for the tool, while your device controls memory and processing speed. For sensitive documents, keep the original and follow your own document-handling rules.
