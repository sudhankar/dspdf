How to enhance a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the upload box.
  2. Choose an enhancement mode such as Automatic, Grayscale, Black & White, Color Boost, High Contrast or Sharpen.
  3. Adjust brightness and contrast if needed.
  4. Use the eraser on the preview to cover unwanted marks and use the crop sliders to trim page edges.
  5. Choose Current page, a Range, or All pages.
  6. Preview the result, then click Apply Enhancement & Download.

What Enhance PDF can do

Scan cleanup

Automatic enhancement, brightness, contrast and sharpening can improve readability of scanned pages.

Color control

Convert to grayscale or black and white, or boost color for image-heavy documents.

Remove unwanted areas

Use the eraser to whiten selected preview areas and crop margins from each page.

Technical limitations

  • The enhanced output is rasterized page content, so the original selectable text layer may not be preserved.
  • Higher output DPI improves detail but uses more memory and can make processing slower.
  • Eraser marks are white raster areas; they are not a secure redaction method.
  • Crop values are percentages and are applied consistently to the selected pages.

Frequently asked questions

Is Enhance PDF free?

Yes. DSPDF provides the tool without an account and processes the document locally in the browser.

Which mode should I use for a scanned document?

Automatic Enhance is a useful starting point. Grayscale or Black & White can help with monochrome scans, while Color Boost is intended for color documents.

Can I adjust brightness and contrast?

Yes. Both controls can be adjusted and previewed before export.

Can I remove stains or unwanted marks?

The eraser can whiten selected areas on the preview. It should not be treated as secure document redaction.

Can I crop the enhanced PDF?

Yes. Set Top, Right, Bottom and Left crop percentages and preview the result before applying it.

Can I enhance selected pages only?

Yes. Choose the current page, a range, or all pages.

Does the PDF stay on my device?

Yes. Enhancement runs in your browser and the PDF is not uploaded to a DSPDF server.

Will text remain selectable?

Enhancement is image-based, so selectable text from the original PDF may not remain in the enhanced output.

Privacy

Enhance PDF processes the selected PDF in your browser rather than sending it to a DSPDF PDF-processing server. Your browser may load public libraries needed for the tool, while your device controls memory and processing speed. For sensitive documents, keep the original and follow your own document-handling rules.
