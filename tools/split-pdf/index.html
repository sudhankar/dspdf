Split PDF

Break one PDF into several. Choose "every N pages" or specify exact ranges — output is delivered as a ZIP.

🔒 Processed locally in your browser.

Drop a PDF here or click to select

Choose the right way to split a PDF

Splitting is useful when one PDF contains several independent documents or when you need to send different page ranges to different people. Use a custom range such as 1-3, 5, 7-10 when you know exactly which pages belong together, or use the automatic every-N-pages option for repeated sections.

If you only need a few selected pages in one new PDF, Extract Pages is simpler. If your goal is to remove unwanted pages while keeping the rest as one document, use Delete Pages instead.

How to split a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Choose "Every N pages" or enter custom ranges like 1-3, 5, 7-10.
  3. Click Split & download ZIP.
  4. You receive a ZIP file containing one PDF per chunk.

Technical limitations

  • Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first.
  • Ranges are inclusive of both endpoints. So 1-3 means pages 1, 2, and 3.
  • Bookmarks and form fields are not preserved.

Frequently asked

Do I get one file or many?

Many. They're bundled into a ZIP to save you clicks.

Can I split a protected PDF?

Not directly. Unlock it first with Unlock PDF.

Privacy

Split PDF processes the selected PDF in your browser rather than sending it to a DSPDF PDF-processing server. Your browser may load public libraries needed for the tool, while your device controls memory and processing speed. For sensitive documents, keep the original and follow your own document-handling rules.
