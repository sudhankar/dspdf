Protect PDF with Password

Encrypt your PDF with a password. The encryption happens on your device — nothing is uploaded.

🔒 Processed locally in your browser.

⚠ Read this first
The PDF is encrypted locally with AES-256. Your PDF and password are processed in the browser and are not uploaded by this tool.

If you forget the password, the file cannot be recovered. Not by us, not by anyone.

Drop a PDF here or click to select

How to protect a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Type a strong password (12+ characters, mixed case, numbers).
  3. Confirm the password.
  4. Click Encrypt & download.
  5. Save the encrypted PDF and remember the password.

Technical limitations — please read

  • This tool uses AES-256 PDF encryption for the protected output.
  • Password is never stored or transmitted. It's used in-memory during encryption and forgotten when the tab closes.
  • No recovery possible. If you lose the password, the file is unrecoverable. Store it safely.
  • Some PDF readers do not fully respect all encryption flags; some may open the file with limited restrictions regardless.

Frequently asked

Can I recover a forgotten password?

No. PDF encryption is designed to be one-way.

Is my password sent anywhere?

No. It's used only in your browser's memory during the encryption step.

What's a good password?

12+ characters, mixed case, numbers, and symbols. Avoid dictionary words. Use a password manager.

Privacy

Protect PDF with Password processes the selected PDF in your browser rather than sending it to a DSPDF PDF-processing server. Your browser may load public libraries needed for the tool, while your device controls memory and processing speed. For sensitive documents, keep the original and follow your own document-handling rules.
