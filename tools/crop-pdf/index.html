Crop PDF

Trim margins by specifying equal cuts on all sides — the same crop is applied to every page.

🔒 Processed locally in your browser.

Drop a PDF here or click to select

1 inch = 72 points. 1 cm ≈ 28.35 points.

Preview (page 1)

How to crop a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Enter crop amounts in points for each side.
  3. Watch the preview update (page 1 shown).
  4. Click Apply crop & download.

Technical limitations

  • Crop changes the page's visible area (CropBox). Content outside the crop is hidden but still present in the file. To fully remove hidden content, rasterize via the Compress PDF tool or export from the editor.
  • Same crop is applied to every page. For per-page cropping, use the PDF Editor.
  • Extreme crops (larger than half the page) can produce very small visible areas. Preview first.

Frequently asked

Does crop remove hidden content?

No — it hides the area. The underlying data is still in the file unless you rasterize.

Can I crop just one page?

Not with this tool. Use the PDF Editor for per-page crops, or split first.

What units does it use?

PDF points. 72 points = 1 inch. 28.35 points ≈ 1 cm.
