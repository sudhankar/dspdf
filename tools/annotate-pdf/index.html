How to annotate a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Choose a tool: sticky note, stamp, or freehand draw.
  3. Click on the page to place a note or stamp, or drag to draw.
  4. Use Prev / Next to annotate multiple pages.
  5. Click Save annotated PDF.

Technical limitations

  • Notes and stamps are drawn as flat page content — they do not appear in PDF reader "Comments" panels.
  • Annotations cannot be edited or removed after saving. Keep your original if revisions may be needed.
  • Sticky note text is limited to what fits in the note box; long text wraps.

Frequently asked

Are these real PDF comments?

No — they're drawn as page content. They look correct in any PDF reader, but won't show in the interactive comments panel.

Can I remove a note later?

Not from the output PDF. Keep your original file.

What stamps are available?

APPROVED, DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, REVIEWED, PAID, VOID, EXPIRED, SAMPLE — plus a custom option.
