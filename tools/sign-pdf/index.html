How to sign a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Create your signature — draw it, type it, or upload an image.
  3. Drag the signature to the required position and resize it with the handles.
  4. Click Save signed PDF.

Technical limitations

  • Not a cryptographic signature. This is a visual image placed on the page. If your counterparty requires a certified digital signature (e.g., for legal contracts), use a dedicated eSignature platform.
  • The signature image is a PNG. It's embedded in the PDF at the position you click.
  • Signature size and position are fixed at placement time (not editable after saving). For fine adjustments, use the PDF Editor.

Frequently asked

Is this legally valid?

Depends on jurisdiction and what the receiving party accepts. This is a visual signature, not a certificate-based digital signature.

Where is my signature stored?

Only in your browser's memory during this session. Nothing is uploaded. Closing the tab erases it.

Can I sign multiple pages?

Yes — click on the page, save, reopen the file to sign another page. Or use the PDF Editor which allows multiple placements in one session.

Privacy

Sign PDF processes the selected PDF in your browser rather than sending it to a DSPDF PDF-processing server. Your browser may load public libraries needed for the tool, while your device controls memory and processing speed. For sensitive documents, keep the original and follow your own document-handling rules.
