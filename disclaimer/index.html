Nothing on DSPDF constitutes legal, financial, medical, accounting, or other professional advice. Blog articles and tool documentation describe general practices and technical details — they are not a substitute for advice from a qualified professional in your jurisdiction.

Specific examples:

  • Signature tools. Our signature tools produce visual signatures, not cryptographic digital signatures. Whether a visual signature is legally valid for a particular document depends on your jurisdiction and the receiving party. Consult a lawyer for anything that matters.

3. Output accuracy

PDF operations can produce unexpected results due to quirks in the PDF format, bugs in underlying libraries, or unusual input files. We work hard to prevent this, but we cannot guarantee perfection.

Before relying on any output:

  • Open the file and inspect it visually.
  • Check that pages, content, and metadata are as expected.
  • Keep a copy of the original file until you've confirmed the output.
  • Test with a non-critical file first if you're unsure.

4. Compression and quality loss

File compression is inherently lossy. Compressing a PDF may reduce image quality, convert text to images, and remove metadata. This is documented on the relevant tool pages, but please take it seriously: compressed output cannot be restored to original quality.

Always keep your original file.
